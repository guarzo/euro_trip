## Why it matters

Every other question on this site assumes winter break 2028/29 as the window and argues about what to do inside it. None of them ask whether winter break is the right window in the first place. It's worth answering honestly rather than by default, because the answer changes everything downstream of it — the climate tables, the day sketches, and every recommendation on this site are built for a winter trip specifically.

## The options

### 1. Winter break, as planned

Roughly two to three weeks in late December and January, long enough for a real fourteen-day trip without pulling anyone out of school. The tradeoff is the one this whole site is built around: short days, cold weather in the north, and a narrower slice of what these cities can offer compared to their best season.

### 2. Summer

The obvious alternative for weather — every city on this list is warmer, brighter, and longer-lit in July than in January. But summer break is also the season everything else competes for: camps, jobs, and — as Bubu and Gaby get older — internships and their own plans. It's also when Europe is at its most crowded and most expensive, and the Greek islands and the Amalfi Coast, both ruled out for winter, would come back on the table.

### 3. Spring break

A week, sometimes ten days, depending on the school calendar — genuinely pleasant weather in the Mediterranean, and shoulder-season pricing and crowds. The hard constraint is length: a week doesn't support a fourteen-day, three-or-four-base trip. This would mean either a shorter, single-base version of the trip, or ruling this option out on length alone.

### 4. A different long weekend or a dedicated trip outside school breaks

If flexibility exists — a gap year, remote work, a trip that doesn't have to align with anyone's school calendar — the whole premise of "winter break 2028/29" dissolves and the question becomes open-ended. Worth naming as a possibility, even if it's the least likely one for this family.

## My recommendation

**Stay with winter break.** It's the only option on this list that is both long enough for the trip this site describes and actually available without asking anyone to miss school or work. Summer has the weather advantage but not the practical one — it's the season with the most competing claims on everyone's time, and it's also the season when this trip would look completely different (different cities ruled in, different cities ruled out, different pace). Spring solves the crowds-and-weather problem but not the length problem.

The honest case for winter is that it was chosen for a reason before this site existed to argue about it: it is the one window on the calendar that is actually free.

## What would change my mind

- **If the 2028/29 school calendar makes winter break unusually short** — everything here assumes something like the usual two-to-three-week window. If it's cut down, revisit this before anything else.
- **If summer becomes genuinely open** — no camp, no job, no conflicting plans — the weather argument for summer is real and would justify rebuilding this site's recommendations from scratch rather than patching them.
- **If Bubu or Gaby would rather skip a long trip during the school year entirely** — that objection belongs on this page, in the comments below. [What do Bubu and Gaby want?]({{ '/questions/what-kids-want/' | relative_url }}) assumes the trip is happening and asks what they want out of it; it does not ask whether the window is right.
