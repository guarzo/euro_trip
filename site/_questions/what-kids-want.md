## Why it matters

By winter 2027/28, Bubu is 17 and Gaby is 15. That is a different trip from the one we would plan for a 15- and a 13-year-old.

**This question has no options list, because it isn't ours to answer.** It is a question *for them*, and it is on this site so they can answer it directly rather than have it guessed at.

## The questions, for Bubu and Gaby

Answer in the comments below, or tell us in person and we'll write it down. There are no wrong answers and nothing is booked.

1. **Is there a city on the list you would be disappointed to miss?** One name is enough. Browse [the cities]({{ '/cities/' | relative_url }}) first.

2. **Would you rather have four days in one place or two days in two places?** This is the [pace question]({{ '/questions/pace/' | relative_url }}), and you two will feel the packing-and-moving fatigue harder than anyone.

3. **Rank these:** museums · food · nightlife and going out · outdoors and walking · shopping and markets. Honestly, not diplomatically.

4. **Is a ski day appealing?** The Sierra Nevada resort is about 45 minutes from [Granada]({{ '/cities/granada/' | relative_url }}) — a genuinely unusual thing to do on a trip to southern Spain.

5. **Sunshine or cities?** The real trade in [Which arc?]({{ '/questions/which-arc/' | relative_url }}) is Mediterranean daylight versus London, Paris, and Amsterdam in the dark. If you have a strong feeling either way, it plausibly decides the whole trip.

## My recommendation

**Answer this one before the arc gets locked.** Of all the questions here, this is the one most likely to change the answer to every other question, and it is the only one where the rest of us are guessing.

My honest read of the current plan is that the [northern arc]({{ '/questions/which-arc/' | relative_url }}) is the strongest for a 17- and 15-year-old — West End theatre, Camden, the Light Festival, no language barrier — and that the Mediterranean arc is the better trip for the adults. If that read is wrong, this is where to say so.
