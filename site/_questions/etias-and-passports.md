## Why it matters

This is not a preference question. It is homework, and one piece of it is worth doing today rather than in 2027.

**A necessary caveat:** the trip is more than two years out, and entry requirements, fees, and timelines all change. Everything below is what is known now and must be re-checked roughly six months before departure. Treat it as a list of things to track, not a set of instructions to follow yet.

## What is known

### Passports — the part that needs action now

The Schengen area requires a passport valid for **at least three months beyond your planned departure** from the area, and issued within the previous ten years. Most travel guidance advises keeping **six months** of validity as a safety margin, because airlines apply their own stricter checks at the gate and a marginal passport can end a trip before it starts.

**Concrete action: check all four passport expiry dates today.** Anything expiring before mid-2028 should be renewed well ahead — renewal backlogs are unpredictable, and a passport that expires in early 2028 is a problem for a trip in January 2028 even though the dates technically don't overlap.

### ETIAS — the EU authorization

ETIAS is a pre-travel authorization for visa-exempt visitors, including US citizens. It is an online registration completed before departure, tied to the passport, and valid for multiple entries over a period of years. It is expected to be in force well before this trip.

It is **not a visa** and does not involve an interview or an appointment — but it does need to be done in advance, and it is tied to the specific passport, so **renewing a passport after registering means re-registering.** That is an argument for sorting passports first and ETIAS later.

### The UK is separate

**The UK is not in the Schengen area.** Any London leg is a distinct border crossing with its own entry requirements, and the UK operates its own separate authorization scheme rather than participating in ETIAS.

Practically this means: if the itinerary includes London, that is two systems to satisfy rather than one. It is not difficult, but it is easy to overlook when everything else on the trip is a single internal hop. See [Which arc?]({{ '/questions/which-arc/' | relative_url }}) — the northern arc crosses this border, the Mediterranean arc does not.

## My recommendation

**Do one thing now and defer the rest:**

1. **Now:** check all four passport expiry dates and renew anything expiring before mid-2028.
2. **About twelve months out:** re-read the current ETIAS and UK requirements, because this is when the details will be settled and reliable.
3. **About three months out:** complete whatever registrations are then required, after passports are final.

Nothing else here needs a decision. It needs a calendar reminder.

## What would change my mind

Nothing — this is the one page on the site that isn't a preference. The only open variable is whether the itinerary includes the UK, which determines whether we deal with one authorization scheme or two.
